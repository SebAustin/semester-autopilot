HIST 2410 — The Atlantic World, 1450–1850
Hartwell University, Fall 2026
Dr. Miriam Castellanos · Tuesdays and Thursdays, 1:25–2:40, McGraw 165
Office hours Wednesday 2–4pm or by appointment.

About the course: This seminar traces the movement of people, goods, and
ideas across the Atlantic basin from first contact through the age of
revolutions. Weekly readings average 80–120 pages; come prepared to
discuss them.

Your grade rests on four things. Seminar participation, which I take
seriously and weigh at twenty percent of the final grade. Two short
analytical essays (5–6 pages each), each worth fifteen percent: the
first is due in class on Thursday, September 24, and the second on
Thursday, October 29. A primary-source presentation, worth ten percent,
scheduled individually across the semester — sign-up sheet circulates
in week two. Finally, a research paper of 15–18 pages, worth forty
percent of your grade. A one-page proposal with preliminary
bibliography is due November 10; the full paper is due on the last day
of classes, December 8, by 5pm in my mailbox or by email.

Weekly response posts: by 9pm each Monday, post a 250-word response to
the week's readings on the course forum. These are graded
complete/incomplete and folded into participation.

Readings are available on the course reserve. Purchase only: Thornton,
"Africa and Africans in the Making of the Atlantic World" (2nd ed.).

Attendance: more than two unexcused absences will lower your
participation grade by a third of a letter each.

Accommodations: students with documented accommodations should see me
in the first two weeks so we can make arrangements well before the
first essay.
